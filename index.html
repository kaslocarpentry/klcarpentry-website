About us

Kootenay Lake Carpentry is a small, owner-operated team based in Kaslo, building since 2019. We take pride in work that is built right and finished well. We live in Kaslo, so we're often building for neighbours and friends. We want every client to feel comfortable throughout their project and beyond.

Where we work

Kootenay Lake Carpentry is based in Kaslo and works throughout the Kaslo area.

Towns we serve: Kaslo, Ainsworth, Balfour, Lardeau, Meadow Creek and Argenta.

Questions

Where do you work?

Kootenay Lake Carpentry is based in Kaslo, BC and works on Kootenay Lake in the Kaslo area, including Ainsworth, Lardeau and Meadow Creek.

What do you build?

Kootenay Lake Carpentry builds workshops and garages, custom homes and cabins, and foundations. It also does energy efficiency renovations and upgrades on existing buildings, and the occasional unusual project.

Who will I be dealing with?

Kootenay Lake Carpentry is a small, owner-operated team, so you deal directly with the owner from the first call to the finished build.

How do you handle costs?

Kootenay Lake Carpentry tracks costs against an agreed budget during the project, so you know where the money is going.

Can you make my older building more energy efficient?

Yes. Energy efficiency renovations and upgrades are one of the services Kootenay Lake Carpentry offers.

How do I get a quote?

To get a quote from Kootenay Lake Carpentry, call (778) 205-4701 or email cshcarpentry@outlook.com. Tell us what you want built, where it is, and roughly when you would like to start.

Tell us what you're planning.

Call weekdays during business hours. If we are on a job site, leave a message and we will call you back within 2 to 3 days. You can also email us.
